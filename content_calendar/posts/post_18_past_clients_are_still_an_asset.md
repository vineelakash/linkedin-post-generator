## LinkedIn Post Copy

Acquiring a brand new borrower or commercial insurance client costs 5x more than writing new business for someone who already knows and trusts you.

So why do 90% of brokerages and agencies treat closed files like dead archives?

Here is what typically happens after closing:
- Borrower buys a home. LO sends a "Congratulations!" gift basket.
- Total silence for 3 years.
- When the borrower is ready to buy an investment property, take out home equity, or refi, they go to the lender whose ad popped up on their Instagram that morning.

The same thing happens in P&C: an agency writes an auto/home bundle, collects commission for 4 years, and never reaches out to pitch the client's new business liability policy or umbrella policy.

Your past client database is your highest-margin asset.

Here is how event-driven AI monitoring turns past clients into ongoing pipeline:

1. **Trigger Monitoring:** Tracks external and internal trigger events (e.g. rate dip thresholds, property appreciation/equity milestones, policy anniversaries, life changes).
2. **Contextual Touchpoint Generation:** Automatically drafts hyper-relevant, non-spammy check-ins:
   *"Hey Jason, saw that home values in your neighborhood appreciated significantly over the last 24 months. Based on your current balance, you may have enough equity to eliminate PMI or fund that home remodel you mentioned. Want me to run a quick 2-minute numbers breakdown?"*
3. **Warm Response Handling:** Answers initial borrower questions and schedules a review directly on the broker's calendar.

No aggressive sales cold calling. Just proactive, high-value advisory delivered at the exact moment your client needs it.
