## LinkedIn Post Copy

Before you post another job listing for a 65,000 a year Customer Service Representative, take a hard look at what your CSRs actually spend their day doing.

Most agencies assume volume growth means they need more headcount. Usually, what they really need is to fix their workflow.

Walk through a CSR's day. Roughly 30% is re-typing data from emails into their AMS. Another 25% goes to generating the same certificates of insurance over and over. About 20% is hunting down VINs, mortgagee info, and renewal docs. That leaves only about 25% for what actually builds retention—talking to clients, reviewing policies, spotting cross-sell opportunities.

Hire another CSR into that broken setup and you've just added 65,000 in salary, payroll taxes, benefits, and management headaches. The bottleneck stays.

An operations layer built on automation handles the repetitive work—COI generation, ID cards, document pulls, renewal reminders. It frees your current team to do what only people can do: actually serve clients.

Your experienced staff can focus on relationships and revenue instead of paperwork. And the math is simple: your existing team suddenly handles 2.5x the work.

Scale your workflow before you scale your payroll.

What's taking up the most time in your agency right now—the stuff that's eating into client-facing work?
