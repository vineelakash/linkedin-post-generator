## LinkedIn Post Copy

I Wouldn't Automate the Broker.

Anyone saying AI will replace you doesn't understand regulated financial services. Full stop.

A borrower picking a mortgage isn't like buying shoes online. Neither is an insurer getting advice on proper coverage limits for a ten million commercial operation.

People don't want algorithms making life-altering financial decisions. They want you—someone who knows the messy exceptions, who'll advocate for them during underwriting hell, who understands what actually gets approved.

That's the human job: navigating tricky credit guidelines. Advising on coverage gaps. Being in their corner when things get stressful.

AI doesn't touch underwriting approvals, coverage suitability calls, or any regulated lending decision. That's your boundary.

Here's where the line actually lives.

AI runs the admin:
Instant lead intake. Document collection and tracking. Shared inbox triage. Certificate pulls. Stalled deal alerts.

You own the decisions:
Risk counsel. Loan structuring. Pricing strategy. Exception negotiations. Client trust.

The goal isn't to automate the broker. It's to kill the busywork that stops you from being one.

So what's your biggest time drain—the thing that keeps you from actual client work?

Let me know. And if you're thinking about boundaries, send me a DM.
