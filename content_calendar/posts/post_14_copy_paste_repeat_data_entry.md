## LinkedIn Post Copy

Your employees are stuck in purgatory.

PDF on one monitor. CRM on the other. Typing the same numbers over and over. You're paying licensed people to do data entry.

In mortgage, it's the same dance: lead in the CRM, app in POS, docs in the LOS, rates from the pricing engine, quotes scattered across wholesale portals.

Insurance brokers have it just as bad: quote hits email, pulls into the rater, syncs to the AMS, spits out carrier portals. Somewhere in there, something gets re-keyed wrong.

Every handoff adds friction. Typos happen. People burn out.

What if documents just moved between systems on their own?

AI reads the 1003, ACORD, tax returns, bank statements—extracts what actually matters. Data lands correctly formatted in your LOS, your AMS, your underwriting portals. No re-entry. No mistakes.

Your good people focus on what they're actually licensed to do: talking to clients, building relationships, closing deals.
