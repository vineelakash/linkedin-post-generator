## LinkedIn Post Copy

Most brokerages don't have a lead generation problem.

They have a response lag problem.

Here is what happens in almost every independent shop:

1. You spend thousands every month driving inbound inquiries through Google, Meta, or aggregators.
2. An inquiry comes in at 2:15 PM while your loan officer or producer is on a 45-minute consultation.
3. The lead sits untouched in the CRM for 2 hours.
4. When your team finally calls, the prospect doesn't pick up — they already called the next brokerage on Google.

Study after study shows the same data: if you don't respond within 5 minutes, your odds of qualifying that lead drop by 80%. After 30 minutes, you're essentially buying cold data.

Buying more leads to fix a leaky bucket is the most expensive mistake in financial services.

An AI operations layer solves the gap instantly:

- Acknowledges new inquiries across SMS, email, or web chat in under 45 seconds.
- Asks 3-4 preliminary qualifying questions (loan type, property value, timeline, or policy type).
- Drops a calendar link to book directly with your licensed LO or producer.
- Hands off a warm, pre-qualified prospect ready for advice.

No licensed professional gets replaced. You simply remove the latency that kills your ROI.

How fast is your team's average first response to a new inbound lead?
