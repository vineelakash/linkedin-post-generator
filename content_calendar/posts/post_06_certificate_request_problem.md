## LinkedIn Post Copy

One certificate of insurance request takes about 8 minutes to process.

That doesn't sound like much.

Then you multiply it by 500 COI requests a month, which is normal for a busy commercial book.

500 x 8 minutes is 66 hours of manual admin work every month.

That's close to two full weeks of a CSR's salary going toward:

Reading email requests from contractors and lenders.

Copying over holder name, address, and special wording.

Digging through Applied Epic, AMS360, or HawkSoft to find the right policy file.

Checking GL, auto, or umbrella limits against what was requested.

Building the ACORD 25 by hand and emailing it back.

COIs matter. Commercial clients expect fast turnaround, and slow certs cost you renewals.

But none of that requires a licensed CSR's judgment. It's data entry with extra steps.

Here's what an AI layer for certificates actually does:

Reads the incoming email and pulls out holder info, additional insured language, and waiver of subrogation requirements.
Checks the request against your active policy limits inside your AMS.
Pre-fills the ACORD 25 in seconds.
Flags anything unusual (like a requested limit above current coverage) for the CSR to review. Everything else gets approved with one click and sent out automatically.

That's 60+ hours a month back for your CSRs. Time they can spend on renewals, policy reviews, and actually talking to clients instead of retyping the same five fields all day.

Two weeks a month of licensed staff time on certificate data entry isn't a badge of honor. It's a gap in your workflow.

How many COI requests does your team handle in a month? I'd guess most agency principals haven't actually run the math.
