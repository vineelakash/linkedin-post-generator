## LinkedIn Post Copy

The most expensive mortgage leads in your pipeline are the ones you already paid for and abandoned.

Every month, borrowers start an application, fill out 60% of the 1003, hit a friction point, and go completely silent.

They didn't say no.
They didn't go to another lender.
They just got stuck.

Maybe they didn't have their W-2 handy. Maybe they got confused by an asset question. Maybe dinner was ready and they closed the tab.

In most brokerages, those unfinished files sit in Encompass or Point forever.

Let's look at the simple economics of recovering just 5 of them a month:

5 recovered applications
× 50% funded conversion rate
× 6,500 average revenue per funded loan
= 16,250 in recovered revenue every single month.

That is nearly 200,000 a year from borrowers you already paid ad spend to acquire.

Here is how an automated recovery system fixes this:

1. **Detects:** Flags applications with zero activity for 24–48 hours.
2. **Engages:** Sends an omnichannel check-in (SMS/email) asking: *"Hey Alex, noticed you were almost done with the application. Did you get stuck on the employer section or need a hand with docs?"*
3. **Unblocks:** Collects the missing field or answers basic logistical questions via conversational AI.
4. **Handoff:** Hands the completed application back to the Loan Officer ready for credit review and structuring.

You don't need more lead spend. You just need a system that finishes what you started.

How does your team currently follow up on incomplete 1003s?
