## LinkedIn Post Copy

You probably don't need to spend thousands on fresh lead generation this month.

You might just need to turn on the 4,000 contacts gathering dust in your CRM.

In most mortgage brokerages and P&C agencies, the database is a graveyard of past opportunities:
- Inquiries from 9 months ago who weren't ready to buy yet.
- Past closed borrowers whose rate or life situation changed.
- Monoline personal lines clients who never got pitched commercial or umbrella coverage.
- Prospects who requested a quote last year and never bound.

Your producers and loan officers don't have time to manually dial 4,000 cold contacts. Nor should they.

This is where conversational AI reactivation works best:

1. **Intelligent Segmentation:** Groups contacts by loan origination date, policy renewal date, or equity milestones.
2. **Contextual Outreach:** Sends hyper-personalized, 2-line conversational prompts (SMS/Email):
   *"Hey Sarah, with current equity numbers in Austin, many homeowners on your block looked into consolidating higher-interest balances. Want me to run a quick 3-minute equity snapshot for you?"*
3. **Intent Qualification:** Answers incoming replies, handles objections, and filters for real buying intent.
4. **Human Booking:** Books a high-intent strategy call directly into the licensed broker's calendar.

Instead of burning cash on cold aggregator clicks, you generate pipeline from people who already recognize your brand.

Before buying more leads this quarter, check what's sitting in your database.

When was the last time you ran a reactivation campaign on your old contacts?
