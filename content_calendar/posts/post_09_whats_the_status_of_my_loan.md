## LinkedIn Post Copy

Nearly 40% of your daily calls and texts? They're asking the same thing:
"What's the status of my loan?"
"Did underwriting look at my paystubs?"
"When's the appraisal coming?"
"Are we closing Friday?"

Borrowers are anxious. That's normal. Your problem is different.

Your loan officers and processors are spending 2-3 hours every day logging into Encompass or LendingPad, typing status emails, and getting interrupted mid-deal. Each status check pulls them away from structuring deals, building Realtor relationships, or actually moving things forward.

There's a better way.

An AI status layer that syncs with your LOS in real time reads your actual milestones—Appraisal Ordered, Conditional Approval, Clear to Close. When a borrower texts or calls asking for an update, the AI pulls the live file data and answers immediately in plain English:

"Hey Alex! Your appraisal came back approved this morning at valuation. Underwriting is reviewing homeowners insurance now. You're on track for closing next Thursday."

If something needs licensed judgment—a rate lock question or complex condition—the AI flags it to your LO right away with full context. No missed details. No surprises.

Borrowers get peace of mind 24/7. Your team gets their focus back.

What would your LOs do with 2-3 extra hours every day?

DM "STATUS" for the borrower update engine blueprint.
