## LinkedIn Post Copy

Your competitor doesn't need a bigger marketing budget than you.

They just need to answer the prospect when you don't.

Here's the scenario:

A prospective homebuyer gets home from work, puts the kids to bed, and starts researching mortgage rates or homeowners insurance quotes at 9:47 PM.

They find your site, see your number, and call.

What happens?
- It rings 5 times.
- Goes to a generic voicemail: *"Thanks for calling, our office hours are Monday through Friday, 9 to 5..."*
- The caller hangs up without leaving a message.
- They click the very next search result on Google.

Over 35% of high-intent consumer inquiries in financial services occur outside standard business hours. If your response stops when you lock the office door, you're leaking a third of your pipeline.

An AI operations reception layer fixes this 24/7:

- Answers instantly via natural, human-sounding conversational voice or instant SMS.
- Captures preliminary deal parameters (property address, estimated loan balance, coverage needs).
- Checks your team's live calendar and books a morning strategy session for 9:30 AM tomorrow.
- Syncs the structured notes and audio transcript directly to your CRM before your staff even brews their morning coffee.

No missed calls. No lost ad spend. No voicemail graveyards.

What currently happens when a prospect calls your office at 9:47 PM?
