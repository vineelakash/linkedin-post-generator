## LinkedIn Post Copy

The Loan Isn't Lost. It's Stalled.

Loans don't usually die with a "no." They die in silence. Nobody notices until it's too late.

Here's what it looks like: Borrower gets pre-approved, finds a house, goes under contract. Things are moving. Then radio silence.

Day 3: Processor asks for updated paystub and gift letter.
Day 6: Borrower's busy, doesn't respond.
Day 10: Lender condition deadline passes.
Day 14: Rate lock expires. Borrower panics. Starts calling other lenders.

The loan wasn't bad. The file didn't get rejected. It just stalled, and nobody caught it in time. When you've got 60+ active files and your team's focused on what's closing Friday, the stalled ones disappear into the noise.

This is where most brokerages leak deals.

What if something watched every file, 24/7?

It catches files that haven't moved in 3+ days. It pinpoints the exact blocker—waiting on a gift letter, waiting on title, waiting on an underwriter condition. It reaches out to the borrower or third party to unblock it. And it alerts your LO before the rate lock or closing date blows up.

You don't need more deals coming in the top of the funnel. You need to stop bleeding deals out of the middle.
