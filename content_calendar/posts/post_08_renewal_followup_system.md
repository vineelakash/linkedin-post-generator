## LinkedIn Post Copy

If your renewal process starts 10 days before expiration, you're already behind.

Most agencies live with renewals on calendar reminders or spreadsheets. Nobody reaches out until the carrier sends a rate increase. By then, the client is frustrated, comparing quotes with competitors, and your account manager is scrambling to remarket five policies in two days under pure panic.

Renewals aren't surprises. They're predictable events that should never become fire drills.

Here's what a clean renewal rhythm looks like:

90 days out: Automated check-in asks the client about changes—new equipment, revenue shifts, payroll updates, anything that affects coverage. You're not guessing anymore.

60 days out: You've got updated exposure details structured and ready. The producer can start shopping before rates harden.

30 days out: You present a proactive renewal briefing with actual options, not scrambling.

Result: Client renews because they know you're paying attention.

The licensed agent controls strategy, negotiations, and carrier relationships. The system just handles the timeline, the outreach, and getting data in front of you when it matters.
