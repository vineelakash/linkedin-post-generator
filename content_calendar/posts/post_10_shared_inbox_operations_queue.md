## LinkedIn Post Copy

Your shared inbox is chaos disguised as communication.

Every morning, 2+ hours gone. Your best account managers reading emails, digging through AMS360, matching policies, manually assigning tasks. All for emails that should route themselves.

Here's what actually lands in there:

Vehicle additions. Endorsement requests. Billing questions. Loss runs. Claims notices. Cancellation alerts. Certificate requests.

Each one needs intent, context, and an owner. Right now? They sit unread while someone hunts for the policy record.

The fix is routing automation. Not AI fluff—actual inbox logic that:

Reads the email. Classifies what it is (Endorsement, Billing, Claim, COI, etc).

Pulls the data. Grabs policy numbers, names, dates, VINs—whatever's actionable.

Matches the client. Finds the exact account and policy in your system.

Routes it to the right person. Creates a task, fills in the details, assigns it to the account manager who owns that client.

Result: Your inbox becomes a queue. Your team works prioritized tasks instead of managing email chaos.

How much time would your agency get back if endorsements just routed themselves?
