## LinkedIn Post Copy

Your team of four runs on heroics and tribal knowledge. Someone remembers something, someone else yells across the room, and deals close.

Then you double in size.

Leads sit in the CRM untouched for six hours. Documents disappear into shared inboxes. Renewals drop without a second glance. Nobody owns anything, so nobody takes responsibility when it fails.

The problem isn't that you hired the wrong people. It's that you're scaling chaos.

When you try to add headcount to a broken manual process, your margins compress, error rates climb, and your best people leave. The heroics that got you to twenty won't get you to fifty.

Most brokerages skip the operational foundation and jump straight to hiring and marketing. Then they wonder why growth feels like failure.

Before you add team members or ramp your sales machine, you need:

A consistent intake process across every lead channel.

Real-time pipeline visibility so stalled deals surface fast.

Clear task ownership tied to SLA tracking.

Automated document chasing that doesn't depend on someone remembering to follow up.

The operations layer is what makes growth sustainable. Without it, volume breaks you.
