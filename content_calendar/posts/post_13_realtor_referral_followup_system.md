## LinkedIn Post Copy

Your best source of qualified mortgage purchase leads isn't another ad channel.

It's the 35 Realtor partners already sitting in your phone contacts.

Here's what actually happens: Eighty percent of Realtors who closed a deal with you last year will send their next client to someone else if you go quiet. Not because you're bad at your job. Because they literally don't hear from you for months, and the hungry LO down the street won't shut up.

You know this. Your LOs intend to stay in touch.

But the second pipeline volume picks up, every minute gets swallowed by deal firefighting. Partner follow-up disappears. And you're back where you started, fighting for deals that should've been easy.

Relying on memory to manage 30+ referral partners is how purchase pipelines become a slot machine. Unpredictable. Stressful. Exhausting.

An automated system changes that. Real-time updates hit the Realtor's inbox automatically—appraisal in, clear to close, conditional approval. The LO doesn't type a word.

You get alerts when a partner hasn't sent anything in 45 days. Not as a nag. As a reminder.

The system feeds your LO a two-sentence prompt: Hey Marcus, saw you just listed on Elm Street. How are the open houses tracking? Personalized. Timely. Not robotic.

You track which referral sources actually pay. Which don't.

Your Realtors stay informed. They feel like a priority on every deal. They send more deals because you actually stay in their orbit.

Stop losing deals to inconsistency.
