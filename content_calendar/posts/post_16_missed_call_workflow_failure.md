## LinkedIn Post Copy

A missed call isn't an unavoidable phone glitch.

It's a 2,500 commission walking straight across the street to your competitor.

Here's what happens in most agencies:

11:30 AM: All your producers and processors are on appointments, closing calls, or at lunch. A buyer calls your main number. It rings six times and dumps to voicemail. 85% of callers hang up without leaving a message. They click the next lender or agency on Google.

At month-end, you look at 40 missed calls and shrug it off.

It's not background noise. It's revenue walking out the door.

The fix isn't complicated. Treat a missed call like a workflow event, not an accident.

Instant text response. Second that call disconnects, an automated SMS hits their phone: "Hey, this is Alex with [Company]. Sorry we missed you while on a client review—how can we help you today?"

Intent capture. They text back what they need—a pre-approval, a GL quote, whatever.

Quick triage. AI confirms the details and drops a calendar link.

Producer alert. The whole chat history lands on your broker before the prospect even thinks about calling someone else.

You've converted a lost caller into a booked meeting in 90 seconds.

What's the biggest reason you're losing calls right now—staffing gaps, or just no system to catch them when you're in back-to-back meetings?
