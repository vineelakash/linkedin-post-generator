## Detailed Post Specifications

### Post 01: Most Brokerages Don't Have a Lead Problem
* **ICP:** Mortgage + Insurance
* **Hook:** You don't have a lead generation problem. You have a response lag problem.
* **Problem:** Inbound inquiries land while LOs and producers are on calls or out of office. Leads sit for 2–4 hours or overnight. Lead-to-contact rates drop 8x after the first 5 minutes.
* **AI Solution:** Instant AI qualification across SMS/Email/Chat within 60 seconds → books directly on the LO/producer's calendar.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"Your leads aren't the problem."* (Visual: Stream of inquiries entering pipeline, several glowing red/stalled).
  * Infographic: `New Lead → Instant Response (<60s) → Qualification → Calendar Booking → Warm Human Handoff` vs `Manual Lag (3 hrs) → Cold Lead → Competitor`.
  * CTA: *"How many leads are waiting for a response right now?"* / Sub: *"Let's find the leakage."*

### Post 02: The Economics of Recovering 5 Abandoned Applications/Month
* **ICP:** Mortgage
* **Hook:** The most expensive leads in your CRM are the ones you already paid for and abandoned.
* **Formula:** `5 recovered applications × conversion rate (50%) × revenue per funded loan ($6,500) = $16,250/mo in recovered gross revenue.`
* **AI Solution:** Detect application drop-off at 48 hours → personalized SMS/email asking the exact friction point (e.g., missing W2, confusion on rate) → resolve blocker → return warm borrower to LO.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"What if 5 abandoned applications came back every month?"*
  * Infographic: `Abandoned App Detected → Automated Omnichannel Follow-Up → Friction Point Solved → Recovered File → LO Final Approval` with the economics calculation.
  * CTA: *"How much revenue is sitting inside your abandoned applications?"*

### Post 03: Your CRM Might Be Your Biggest Untapped Lead Source
* **ICP:** Mortgage + Insurance
* **Hook:** You don't need to spend \$5,000 on new ads this month. You need to activate the 3,000 contacts in your CRM.
* **Problem:** Thousands of past clients and old inquiries sit dormant in Encompass, Applied Epic, or HubSpot.
* **AI Solution:** AI segments dormant contacts by loan date, policy anniversary, or equity triggers → initiates conversational outreach → qualifies intent → schedules call for broker.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"You might already have thousands of leads."* / Sub: *"You're just not working them."*
  * Infographic: `Dormant Database → AI Segmentation & Triggers → Conversational Re-engagement → Qualified Intent → Booked Review → Broker`.
  * CTA: *"Before buying more leads, check your database."*

### Post 04: Your Competitor Doesn't Need Better Marketing
* **ICP:** Mortgage + Insurance
* **Hook:** Your competitor isn't winning because their ad copy is better. They're winning because they answered at 9:47 PM.
* **Problem:** 35%+ of high-intent mortgage and insurance inquiries happen outside 9-to-5. Missed calls go to voicemail; prospects click the next Google result.
* **AI Solution:** 24/7 AI Voice & SMS receptionist handles inbound, gathers preliminary details, checks eligibility, and books morning consult.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"Your prospect called at 9:47 PM."* (Visual: Timestamp glowing in slate dark UI, missed call turning into lost customer).

### Post 05: Your Processor Shouldn't Be Chasing Documents
* **ICP:** Mortgage
* **Hook:** Your loan processor didn't get licensed and trained to send 40 "Where is your bank statement?" emails a day.
* **Problem:** Manual document chasing consumes 15–20 hours/week per processor. Missing pages, expired paystubs, and blurry scans stall underwriting.
* **AI Solution:** AI checklist engine tracks received vs missing docs → sends automated gentle SMS/Email reminders → auto-classifies uploads → hands clean file to processor.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"Where is the bank statement?"* (Visual: Stalled file badge, checklist with missing icon).
  * Infographic: `Required Checklist → Missing Item Detected → Smart Reminders → Upload Received → AI Classification → Clean File to Processor`.
  * CTA: *"Automate the chase. Keep the decision human."*

### Post 06: The Certificate Request Problem
* **ICP:** Insurance
* **Hook:** One certificate of insurance (COI) request takes 8 minutes. 500 requests a month consume an entire full-time salary.
* **Problem:** Commercial lines agencies drown in routine COI requests. CSRs spend all day typing holder details into Applied Epic/AMS360.
* **AI Solution:** AI inbox parses COI email, extracts holder name/address/wording requirements, validates active policy limits, generates draft/task, and routes to CSR for 1-click issue.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"One certificate request isn't the problem."* / Sub: *"500 of them are."*
  * Infographic: `Email COI Request → AI Extraction → Limit & Coverage Validation → Draft Certificate Generated → CSR 1-Click Verification → Sent`.
  * CTA: *"How many hours does your team spend on certificates?"*

### Post 07: The Loan Isn't Lost. It's Stalled.

* **AI Solution:** Continuous pipeline health monitoring flags any file with zero status change for >72 hours, initiates friendly borrower check-ins, and escalates to LO.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"Your pipeline has silent failures."* (Visual: 5 active pipeline tracks, one fading into stagnant status).

* **AI Solution:** 90-60-30 day automated renewal workflows collect updated payroll/vehicle/property data from the insured, compile a renewal briefing, and present it to the account manager.
* **Images:**
  * Hero: *"Renewals shouldn't start with a reminder on someone's calendar."*
