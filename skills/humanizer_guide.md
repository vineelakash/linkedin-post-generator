# LinkedIn Humanizer & Copywriting Guidelines

## 1. Tone and Voice
* **Peer-to-Peer:** Write like an experienced founder talking directly to a fellow brokerage owner or agency principal.
* **Direct & Empirical:** Focus on practical numbers, real workflows (Encompass, Applied Epic, ACORD forms, 1003s), and concrete operational mechanics.
* **No Fluff or Platitudes:** Eliminate empty phrases like *"In today's fast-paced digital world"*, *"AI is revolutionizing the industry"*, or *"Unlock your potential"*.

---

## 2. Formatting Rules for High LinkedIn Readability
* **One Idea Per Paragraph:** Max 1–3 lines per paragraph to prevent wall-of-text fatigue on mobile screens.
* **Punchy Openers:** First 2 lines must stop the scroll before the "...see more" fold.
* **Clean Lists:** Use bullet points (`-`) or short numbered steps when breaking down multi-step loops.
* **No Hashtag Spam:** 0 to 3 relevant hashtags at most at the bottom (or omit entirely for a cleaner aesthetic).
* **Specific Call-To-Action:** Every post ends with an engaging diagnostic question and a distinct 1-word DM trigger keyword (e.g. `DM "LEAD"`, `DM "RECOVER"`).

---

## 3. The Humanizer Banned Vocabulary (Never Use)

| Banned AI Cliches | What to Use Instead |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Game-changer / Supercharge" | "Fixes", "Improves", "Increases pull-through" |
| "Revolutionize / Transform" | "Automates the manual chase", "Removes latency" |
| "In the ever-evolving landscape" | "In modern mortgage brokerages..." |
| "Delve into / Dive deep" | "Look closely at the numbers", "Break down" |
| "Synergy / Holistically" | "Across your tech stack", "End-to-end" |
| "Seamless / Frictionless" | "Without manual re-entry", "Directly into your LOS" |
| "Tapestry / Testament" | Delete completely |

---

## 4. Pre-Publishing Checklist

- [ ] Does the hook create genuine curiosity without clickbait?
- [ ] Is the operational problem specific to Mortgage or Insurance?
- [ ] Does the copy clearly state that licensed staff retain decision-making authority?
- [ ] Is there a clear, single DM keyword in quotes?
- [ ] Are all 3 visual assets (Hero, Infographic, CTA) attached in correct sequence?
